# Assignment 2: AI Around Me

## Overview
A document exploring 10 real-life AI applications that are used in daily life.

## Content
This assignment identifies and explains how AI and machine learning are integrated into everyday applications:

1. **Google Search** - ML-based ranking and relevance
2. **YouTube Recommendations** - Personalized video suggestions
3. **Instagram Feed & Reels** - Content recommendation system
4. **Google Maps Navigation** - Traffic prediction and route optimization
5. **Voice Assistants** - Speech recognition and NLP
6. **Gmail Spam Detection** - Email classification
7. **E-commerce Recommendations** - Product suggestions
8. **Predictive Text** - Keyboard auto-correct
9. **Face Unlock** - Computer vision and deep learning
10. **Music Apps** - Personalized music recommendations

## Learning Objectives
- Understand how AI is embedded in everyday technology
- Recognize different types of ML algorithms in real applications
- Appreciate the impact of AI on user experience
