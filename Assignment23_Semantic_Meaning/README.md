# Assignment 23: Semantic Meaning

## Overview
An analysis of semantic similarity between word pairs, exploring how words relate to each other in meaning and their importance in Natural Language Processing.

## What is Semantic Similarity?
Semantic similarity refers to how closely related two words are in meaning. It's a fundamental concept in NLP that helps machines understand language context and relationships.

## Types of Semantic Relationships
- **Synonymy**: Similar meanings (happy, joyful)
- **Antonymy**: Opposite meanings (hot, cold)
- **Hypernymy**: General term (vehicle)
- **Hyponymy**: Specific term (car)
- **Meronymy**: Part-whole (wheel, car)
- **Homonymy**: Same word, different meanings (bank)
