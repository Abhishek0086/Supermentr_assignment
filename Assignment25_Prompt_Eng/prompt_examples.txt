# Assignment 25: Prompt Engineering - Detailed Examples

## 1. Introduction
Prompt engineering is the process of designing effective inputs (prompts) to get accurate and useful responses from AI models. A well-structured prompt gives better results than a vague one.

## 2. Objective
To compare weak and strong prompts and understand how prompt design affects AI output.

## 3. Prompt Comparisons

### A. Resume Generation

#### Weak Prompt:
"Make my resume"

**Problems:**
* Too vague
* No details provided
* No format specified
* No context about experience
* Ambiguous expectations

**Expected Output Quality:** Generic, incomplete, may miss important details

---

#### Strong Prompt:
"Create a professional resume for a 3rd-year Computer Science student skilled in Python, JavaScript, React, and AWS. Include projects, skills, and internship experience in a clean format."

**Improvements:**
* Clear role
* Specific skills
* Structured output
* Experience level defined
* Format specification

**Expected Output Quality:** Targeted, detailed, well-organized

---

### B. Business Idea

#### Weak Prompt:
"Give me a business idea"

**Problems:**
* No domain or constraints
* Generic output
* No target audience
* No scalability considerations
* Vague requirements

**Expected Output Quality:** Generic, may not be actionable

---

#### Strong Prompt:
"Suggest a unique startup idea in the food industry targeting college students in India, focusing on low cost and high scalability. Include revenue model and competitive advantage."

**Improvements:**
* Target audience
* Domain specified
* Constraints included
* Geographic focus
* Additional requirements

**Expected Output Quality:** Specific, actionable, tailored

---

### C. Study Plan

#### Weak Prompt:
"Make a study plan"

**Problems:**
* No subject or timeline
* No skill level mentioned
* Ambiguous structure
* No learning objectives
* Unclear duration

**Expected Output Quality:** Generic, may not match needs

---

#### Strong Prompt:
"Create a 30-day study plan for learning Python and cloud computing for a beginner, including daily topics and practice tasks."

**Improvements:**
* Time-bound
* Subjects defined
* Structured output
* Skill level specified
* Clear components

**Expected Output Quality:** Structured, actionable, tailored

---

## 4. Key Differences

### Weak Prompts:
* Vague and unclear
* Missing context
* No constraints
* Ambiguous goals
* Generic output

### Strong Prompts:
* Specific and detailed
* Comprehensive context
* Well-defined constraints
* Clear objectives
* Targeted output

---

## 5. Additional Prompt Engineering Examples

### Example 1: Content Writing

**Weak:** "Write an article"

**Strong:** "Write a 1000-word blog post about AI in healthcare for a non-technical audience. Include 3 real-world examples, benefits, and challenges. Use simple language and include a conclusion with future outlook."

---

### Example 2: Code Generation

**Weak:** "Write a function"

**Strong:** "Write a Python function that takes a list of numbers and returns the sum of all even numbers. Include error handling for non-numeric values and add docstring with examples."

---

### Example 3: Marketing Copy

**Weak:** "Create marketing text"

**Strong:** "Create a 150-word marketing copy for a fitness app targeting busy professionals aged 25-40. Highlight time-saving features, health benefits, and include a call-to-action. Use persuasive but professional tone."

---

### Example 4: Project Planning

**Weak:** "Make a project plan"

**Strong:** "Create a 3-month project plan for developing a mobile app with a team of 4 developers. Include milestones, deliverables, resource allocation, and risk management. Format as a Gantt chart outline."

---

### Example 5: Learning Material

**Weak:** "Explain machine learning"

**Strong:** "Explain machine learning concepts for a beginner with no programming background. Use 3 real-world analogies, keep it under 500 words, and include a simple example with step-by-step explanation."

---

## 6. Prompt Engineering Techniques

### Technique 1: Specificity
Add details about what you want:
- Who is the audience?
- What is the purpose?
- What format do you need?
- What constraints exist?

### Technique 2: Context Provision
Explain the background:
- Why do you need this?
- What will it be used for?
- What is the current situation?
- What are the constraints?

### Technique 3: Format Definition
Specify the output structure:
- Bullet points or paragraphs?
- Length requirements?
- Specific sections?
- Visual format?

### Technique 4: Example Provision
Show what you want:
- Provide sample outputs
- Show desired style
- Clarify expectations
- Demonstrate format

### Technique 5: Constraint Setting
Define limitations:
- Budget or cost
- Time constraints
- Audience level
- Technical requirements

---

## 7. Conclusion
Prompt engineering plays a crucial role in getting accurate results from AI systems. Well-designed prompts improve output quality and efficiency.

### Key Takeaways:
1. Specificity matters - vague prompts get vague results
2. Context helps AI understand your needs
3. Clear constraints improve relevance
4. Format specification ensures usable output
5. Examples guide the AI toward desired results

### Best Practices:
- Be specific and detailed
- Provide relevant context
- Define output format clearly
- Set constraints and goals
- Iterate and refine based on results
- Use examples when possible
- Ask for reasoning when needed

### Practice Tips:
- Compare weak and strong prompts
- Test different phrasings
- Analyze output quality
- Refine based on results
- Share and discuss with others
- Build a library of effective prompts
